About Me

Building intelligent systems at the intersection of AI and software engineering

AI Research Meets Real-World Engineering

I'm a graduate Computer Science student at Northeastern University focused on building intelligent systems that combine AI research with real-world software engineering. My work sits at the intersection of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Large Language Models, and scalable full-stack development, with a strong interest in creating practical AI products that solve meaningful problems.

I've worked on projects involving anomaly detection, autonomous vehicle systems, multi-agent AI workflows, RAG pipelines, and AI-powered platforms using modern LLM ecosystems. One of my major research-focused projects explores secure Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication for autonomous vehicles using blockchain, AI-driven anomaly detection, and simulation environments like SUMO. I've also built AI applications leveraging IBM Granite models, retrieval systems, and agentic workflows to improve decision-making and automation.

On the software engineering side, I enjoy building end-to-end applications across the MERN stack and modern cloud-native tooling. My experience includes backend architecture, API development, authentication systems, databases, and deploying scalable applications with clean, maintainable code. I'm particularly interested in combining strong engineering principles with AI systems to build products that are both technically robust and impactful.

I enjoy experimenting with emerging AI technologies, exploring how LLMs can enhance developer productivity and user experiences, and continuously learning through research, hackathons, and hands-on projects. My goal is to contribute to the next generation of intelligent software systems that bridge research and production-ready engineering.
